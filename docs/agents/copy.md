# Concise copy on marketing pages and docs

People skim the public site on a phone. A wall of text there means the copy is wrong, however accurate it is. Write the least that makes the point, and cut before you add.

- Marketing pages: a tile, bullet, step or card is a bold label plus a sentence or two, about 20 words at most. Section leads and page descriptions stay under about 28 words. Prefer a big number, a short label or a drawn scene over a sentence that explains them. One idea per item. Don't restate the heading in the text below it, and don't walk through settings or button sequences; that belongs in the docs.
- Docs: each paragraph makes one point in about 60 words or fewer, and each step is one action in about 50 words or fewer. Figure captions stay under about 25 words. Give the real button labels and facts, and prefer steps or a short list over a long paragraph.
- Long-form pages keep their own shape: blog posts, the changelog and legal pages.
- The limits are a ceiling, not a target.
